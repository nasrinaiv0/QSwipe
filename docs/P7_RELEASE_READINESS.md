## Architecture and accounting
Production root uses src/state/store.ts localStorage, previewId and local canAccess; src/views/attendance.ts and src/qr/flows.ts use local QR and attendance, src/views/reports.ts exports local data, src/views/settings.ts can import/export/clear local audit. The staging shell is a separate UI, not a production adapter. lib/staging/security.js intentionally rejects VERCEL_ENV=production even with flags set. Do NOT bypass that guard to release. A production-specific server-authorized surface, authenticated onboarding, scheduling, swipe/check-in and reports, import policy and separation of demo storage/origin must be designed, tested and approved first.
P3 initial mark unique per session/roster, server time, version 1, atomic QR consumption plus event; P4 approved decisions advance projection exactly once, rejection leaves state unchanged, original event immutable to app roles. P5/P6 correction outbox is not an attendance record and must never be counted as one. Reconcile original event + ordered approved decisions to current status/version/recorded_at; ensure no missing/extra events, version gaps, invalid prior state, repeated approval, consumed QR without matching QR event, wrong maker/checker or cross-scope pair. Test negative controls in disposable PG only. Reports must separate scheduled, present, absent, unmarked, excused/leave if later implemented. Present rate = present / (present+absent), null when denominator 0; completion = marked/scheduled. Never infer absent from unmarked. Historical eligibility snapshot, scheduled denominator, overnight shift and IANA zone, leave and payroll rules still missing from production adapter. Do not treat attendance status as hours worked or wages.
